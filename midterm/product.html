Description

A lightweight everyday smartwatch with a bright 1.4-inch screen, all-day heart-rate tracking and up to seven days of battery life. It shows calls and message alerts from your phone and counts your steps automatically.

  • 1.4-inch colour display, readable in sunlight
  • Heart-rate, blood-oxygen and step tracking
  • Water resistant for rain, handwashing and workouts
  • Works with Android and iPhone

Health tracking

The optical sensor on the back checks your heart rate every few minutes, day and night. The watch warns you if your heart rate stays unusually high while you are resting, and the blood-oxygen reading can be taken at any time from the health screen. Sleep tracking shows how long you slept and how much of it was light, deep and REM sleep.

Battery and charging

With normal use the battery lasts up to seven days. Turning on the always-on display or continuous heart-rate tracking shortens this to around three days. The magnetic charger clips onto the back of the watch and charges it fully in about two hours.

Notifications

Once paired with the free companion app, the watch vibrates for calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages. You can read short messages on the screen, reject calls with one tap, and set a silent alarm that wakes you up without disturbing anyone else.
